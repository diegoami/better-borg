## #53: the cause, and what it hid

Not code: **player consent.** The web host attaches `mod.truth` exactly
when the loader granted `state:monsters-actual.read` or
`state:map-actual.read` (neo-angband `packages/web/src/mod-tier.ts:523`,
`mod-api2-host.ts:1003`). Grants are what the mod declared, minus what its
tier may not hold, minus what the player refused or has not decided
(mod-tier.ts:21-27). The lab profile never granted truth, **nor
`session:control`**: the 2026-11-04 lab log shows two characters attaining
c2d1 and NOT kept. The 0.1.0 lab check was accepted on that. The CHANGELOG
and README now say so (bad2b82). The fix is the owner's, once per profile:
Mods -> Mandos -> Change permissions. The prevention is `node tools/lab.mjs
check` (9b9fe6c), and process §8 requires it to exit 0.
