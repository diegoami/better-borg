## Semantics

`junk-space.ts` restores borg_drop_hole (junk.c:514), borg_drop_slow
(:959), borg_dump_quiver (:1154), and their borg_consume helper (:74).
The C's danger/full-HP exception, whole-stack vs one-item quantities,
value/power/weight ties, class/stat potion guards and even its high-level
monetary OR quirks are retained. Existing ItemDeps knowledge seams remain;
unknown book/random-ego knowledge uses conservative defaults. Missing
loadout simulation declines a speculative drop.

`borgWearRecharge` restores wear.c:1449, including the literal rod guard
(the C comment describes a narrower rule). The C queues wear followed by
R75; ThinkSession queues that rest before another ladder decision, so an
earlier wear rung cannot immediately undo the recharge swap.

The known quiver accessor is exposed only to select membership. The raw
inventory already contains those handles; it is never augmented with them.
Pack capacity subtracts actual occupied quiver slots and excludes their
handles from individual pack slots (stock 40/5 accounting). #72's premature
ammo-blindness attribution was corrected and closed, with no such change.

All four rungs occupy their C ladder sites. New drops reuse the existing
junk-kind memory, the port's substitute for the C's borg-ignore inscription,
so dropped stacks are not immediately collected again. Consuming an item
does not mark its kind as junk. Ring swapping remains #4's separate scope.
