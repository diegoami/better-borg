# B56 Diagnose stalls like deaths: one stall watch, a stall record, a stall replay

- **Kind**: CHOICE (FORK-marked). Upstream's port has the tick's guard but no
  shared watch, no headless stall and no stall record. It also fixes a
  DEFECT in our own instrument: the headless harness lacked the tick's
  forget step, so lab and harness played differently whenever a command
  repeated 6 times.
- **Issue**: #56 · **Branch**: worked on `main` (process: a small task, merged on
  verification) · **Merge after**: db55dff
- **Owner (2026-11-04)**: "in the diagnosis we need to see also the scenarios
  where the borg get stuck". This is step 1 of the approved pivot to porting
  errors (#34's comment).

## Done-whens, as built

1. **One stall watch** (`src/loop/stall.ts`): `sceneKey` (the player's grid,
   depth, hp, sp, exp, gold, and each perceived monster's grid and hp, but
   not the game turn, so a rest that changes nothing else is a stall),
   `createStallWatch` (repeat count; forget every 6; stalled at 40 repeats or
   25 idle waits), `aimedGrid` and `forgetGrid`. The tick
   (`src/api2/controller.ts`) now counts through it, with identical
   behaviour: its 17 tests pass unchanged, and the constants are re-exported.
   `plugin.ts`'s inline forget is `forgetGrid`.
2. **The headless harness watches too** (`observingLife`). It applies the
   forget step exactly as the tick does, and at a stall it records
   `{ kind, command, decision, turn }` and ends the life after that decision.
   The census still balances (the stalling command was issued and counted).
3. **The record**: `stall` is an additive optional field (rule 8). It is
   written only for a life that stalled, so every other record is
   byte-identical. `runs.mjs` prints `STALLED (repeat rest at decision 44)`.
4. **The replay**: `tools/replay.mjs` takes dead OR stalled runs, each
   through its recorded class's pack (`packForClass`). A stalled record must
   replay to the same stall (kind, command, decision, turn); the rungs anchor
   at the life's end, as for a death.
5. **The lab**: `tools/lab.mjs check` counts the tick's stall releases.

## Verification

- `src/loop/stall.test.ts`: repeat stall at exactly 40; forget at 6/12/18;
  no stall while the scene changes; idle stall at 25 with a reset; the scene
  key ignores the turn and sees hp; aim and forget.
- **The proof on the real engine**, with the B55 Blackguard fix reinstated
  as a bug (`playerHas` forced false, then restored):
  `runs.mjs --class Blackguard --seeds 1 --cap 400` ends the life at
  decision 44 as `STALLED (repeat rest)`, with the record holding
  `{"kind":"repeat","command":"rest","decision":44,"turn":440}`. Before
  B56 the same life rested to its cap (397 of 400). `replay.mjs` on that
  record reports "reproduced the recorded stall exactly" and writes the
  offset-10 dossier.
- **c2d1 cohort** (seeds 20, 23, 34, 49, cap 10000, stage 2,1): identical to
  the B54 record in every per-run field except the wall clock. The headless
  forget step moves no decision there.
- The full `CI=1 pnpm verify` result is in the commit.
