Kind: inherited DEFECT, toward the C. Issue #68.

## Attribution

The Necromancer starts without a torch (the published class data) and has
UNLIGHT. Its BI_LIGHT is nevertheless 1, in both trait.c:1765 and the port,
to represent its vision. `borg_update_light` (borg-cave-light.c:96) checks
whether INVEN_LIGHT contains an item before marking any BORG_LIGHT grids.
The port instead checked BI_LIGHT > 0, so it invented torchlight and added
25 to the failure rate of every Necromancer spell even in dark corridors.

Two related formula defects are restored toward borg-magic.c:525-534:
PF_ZERO_FAIL comes from declared player flags (Necromancers have it too),
and the lit-grid predicate tests BORG_LIGHT alone, not BORG_GLOW as well.
All three errors exist at import `70145c5` and upstream master `1fa2f8d`.
This corrects #68's earlier provisional attribution of the 44% rate as
faithful: the arithmetic +25 exists in the C, but its predicate differs.
