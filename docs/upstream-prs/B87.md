# Prepared C bug report: west-side light coverage

Intended repository: `angband/angband`. Credit for the finding belongs to
the owner's Rust port. This draft does not request a behavior change in
borg-rs, whose parity contract intentionally retains C quirks.

## Proposed title

Borg: radius-two light misses the two west shoulder grids

## Proposed body

`borg_update_light()` in `src/borg/borg-cave-light.c:137-141` repeats the
west corners instead of marking the west shoulders. Verified on master
`e473720ed88a0f0c7ca66815cef3000eb4a38bc3`.

The east arm marks `(y,x+2)` and `(y+/-1,x+2)`. The west arm instead marks
`(y,x-2)` and `(y+/-2,x-2)`. Those latter corners are already appended by
the north/south arms in an open room, while `(y+/-1,x-2)` remain unlit.
The radius-three-and-up scan skips the central five-by-five box, so it
does not fill these missing shoulder flags either.

A small fixture invoking the unchanged reference function and its macro,
with a radius-two lamp, a player well away from map boundaries and all
floor predicates true, yields 21 list entries but 19 distinct lit grids:
both east shoulders have `BORG_LIGHT`, both west shoulders lack it.
Changing only the two west y offsets from +/-2 to +/-1 yields 21 distinct
lit grids with both shoulders lit on each side. The west floor gate stays.

Suggested correction: mirror the east arm's +/-1 offsets in the west
arm. This does not address duplicate entries in other radius branches.

Originally documented in [diegoami/borg-rs's C bug catalogue, entry 4](https://github.com/diegoami/borg-rs/blob/613227bc53c4f0e77ec457d5721febe9cf8bfe03/docs/c-bugs.md#4-west-of-the-player-lights-the-wrong-grids).
The Rust port deliberately preserves the C behavior for parity. Mandos
tracks its own marked correction in [#87](https://github.com/diegoami/mandos/issues/87);
normal-perception regressions fail before and pass after at radii 2, 3
and 5, with a wall-gating control.

No particular live death is attributed to this error; the reproduced
consequence is incorrect perceived torchlight.
